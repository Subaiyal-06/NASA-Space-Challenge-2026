=======================================================
PART A: DECISIONS MADE BEFORE CODING
=======================================================

D1. Project chosen: "Earth Jukebox: Pakistan" (over the junior astronaut trainer game).
    Why: direct match with NASA data use, local story, less scope risk, accessibility angle.

D2. Core idea: hear Pakistan's climate history as music, month by month, with weather
    animations, a tension meter, and story markers.

D3. Data reality check (important):
      - Temperature (NASA GISTEMP): monthly from 1880, coarse grid, anomalies only.
      - Temperature/rain/drought/air detail (NASA POWER, MERRA-2): from about 1980.
      - Rain (GPM IMERG): from 2000.  Aerosol (MODIS): from 2000.
      - Earthquakes: USGS (not NASA).   Future: NASA NEX-GDDP-CMIP6.
    Consequence: the timeline starts early for temperature and quakes; other layers fade in
    later ("the orchestra grows").

D4. Platform: desktop app with Electron (not a website). A hosted web build is kept only as a
    backup link for judges, plus a demo video.

D5. Timeline: one month = 2.5 seconds (96 BPM, 4 beats per bar). Three modes:
    Month Mode, Year Mode, Story Mode (with smart pacing).

D6. Music: A minor, Interstellar-inspired style (not copying anything), layers driven by a
    tension index. Chords: calm Am-F-C-G, tense Am-F-Dm-E, one chord per 3 months.

D7. Removed features: uncertainty sound, Urdu toggle.

D8. Added features: globe intro, weather animations, ambient edge glow, ghost weather,
    smart pacing, record badges.

D9. Weather visuals use values COMPARED TO NORMAL (anomalies), not raw values.

D10. Honesty rules: label projections, label USGS as non-NASA, call the tension index "our own
     index", and word records "in our data".

D11. Team work: two developers use Claude Code. Developer 1 = engine (data, sound,
     packaging). Developer 2 = experience (globe, weather, glow, UI, accessibility).
     They meet at three contracts: the data format, the global clock, the audio API.

D12. Learning rule: every step ends with a lesson and a log entry (CLAUDE.md).
